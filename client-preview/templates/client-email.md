# Client preview email

Fill the `{placeholders}`. Keep it short: the client should know what to look at and what to answer in under a minute. Send the passcode separately.

## Default: reply with numbered notes

**Subject:** {Project} design preview, ready for your thoughts

Hi {name},

The first look at {project} is ready: {link}

I'll text you the passcode separately. It works on phone and computer.

This is a draft to choose a direction, so the {images/prices/text} are placeholders. {If options: Use the bar at the bottom to switch between designs, fonts and colours.}

Could you reply with numbered notes on:

1. {Which direction feels most like you, and why?}
2. {Anything that feels off or missing?}
3. {One specific question, for example: is the gallery easy to browse on your phone?}

If you can reply by {day, date}, I'll have the next round to you by {date}.

Thanks,
{sender}

## Passcode message (text or other channel)

Hi {name}, the passcode for the {project} preview I just emailed: {passcode}

## Variant: book a call

Replace the numbered questions with:

> Have a look before we talk, then pick a 20-minute slot here: {booking link}. I'll take notes so you don't have to write anything.

## Variant: comments on the page

Only if the client already has an account on the comment tool (for example Vercel toolbar). Replace the numbered questions with:

> You can leave comments right on the page: click the speech bubble at the bottom, then click anything you want to comment on.
