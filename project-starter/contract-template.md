# Website development agreement

**Between** {Your name / business}, {NZBN}, {email} ("I", "me")
**and** {Client business}, {NZBN}, {contact name, email} ("you")
**Date:** {YYYY-MM-DD} · **Quote:** {quote reference}

## 1. The project
I'll design and build {one-line project summary} for you. The features in scope are the MVP scope in the attached PRD (F1–F{n}). Anything not listed there is out of scope, including {e.g. copywriting, photography, ongoing SEO, paid ads}.

## 2. What you'll do
- Send content (copy, photos, logo, prices) by {date}, and give feedback or approvals within {5} business days.
- Complete the steps on the client list I send you: confirm the domain email, set up Stripe, and anything else that needs your identity or logins.
- If your content, approvals or access are late, the timeline moves by the same amount.

## 3. Timeline
I aim to launch by {date}, with these milestones: {milestones}. I'll tell you as soon as I know of a delay on my side.

## 4. Fees and payment
- **Fixed fee:** {amount} + GST.
- **Schedule:** {50%} deposit before work starts, {50%} at launch. {Or: milestone payments.}
- Invoices are due within {14} days. If a payment is more than {14} days late, I may pause work until it's paid.
- The deposit covers my booked time and isn't refundable once work has started.

## 5. Changes
The fee includes {2} rounds of revisions per milestone. New features or changes to the agreed scope are quoted separately, and I start them once you approve the quote in writing.

## 6. Third-party services
The site uses third-party services such as {Cloudflare, Neon, Resend, Stripe, domain registrar}.
- **Running costs:** {One-off: after launch, you pay these providers directly. | Hosting: covered by my hosting fee of {amount} per {year/month}.}
- Their prices and terms can change, and they're outside my control. I'm not responsible for their outages or changes, but I'll help you work around them at my usual rate.

## 7. Accounts and ownership
- **Domain:** registered in your name as owner. I may buy and renew it for you, and I'll transfer it to your account on request.
- **Stripe** and any account holding your customers' personal data are in your name.
- I build on my own GitHub, Cloudflare and Resend accounts. {One-off: I transfer the repo and hosting project to your accounts at launch. | Hosting: they stay on my accounts while you're on the hosting plan, and I transfer them when you leave.}

## 8. Intellectual property
- Once you've paid in full, you own the custom design, code and content made for this project.
- I keep ownership of my pre-existing tools, starter templates and reusable code, and give you a permanent licence to use them as part of your site.
- Open-source components stay under their own licences.
- Until you've paid in full, I own the work and you may not use it.

## 9. Portfolio
I may show the finished work in my portfolio and mention you as a client, unless you tell me not to in writing.

## 10. Launch and warranty
- Before launch I test on current versions of Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge, at phone, tablet and desktop widths.
- For {30} days after launch I'll fix bugs in the work I built at no charge. This doesn't cover new features, content changes, or problems caused by changes made by someone else.

## 11. Ongoing support
Support after the warranty is optional and separate: {care plan of {hours} per month at {amount} + GST | hourly at {rate} + GST}.

## 12. Confidentiality and privacy
We'll each keep the other's confidential information private. You're responsible for how your business collects and uses customers' personal data, including your privacy policy. I'll handle any personal data I access only to do this work, and keep it secure.

## 13. Liability
- My total liability under this agreement is limited to the fees you've paid me.
- Neither of us is liable for indirect losses such as lost profits or data.
- You're acquiring these services for business purposes, so we agree the Consumer Guarantees Act 1993 doesn't apply. {Lawyer to confirm.}

## 14. Ending the agreement
- Either of us may end this agreement with {14} days' written notice.
- If it ends early, you pay for the work done up to that point. Ownership of that work passes to you once it's paid.
- If you go quiet for more than {30} days, I may close the project and invoice the work done.

## 15. Law and disputes
New Zealand law applies. If we disagree, we'll first try to sort it out by talking in good faith. If that fails, either of us may refer it to mediation before going to court.
